## Contribution ladder

CardanoInterface follows a contribution ladder so roles are auditable and
promotion is transparent. See the [Contribution Ladder Framework](https://committees.docs.intersectmbo.org/intersect-open-source-committee/policies/contribution-ladder-framework)
for the definitions used by Intersect's Open Source Committee.

| Level | What it means | Promotion criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Contributor | Submits patches, issues, or reviews | One merged contribution; follows `CONTRIBUTING.md` standards |
| Trusted Committer | Sustained, non-trivial contributions; can merge with review | ~3 months of consistent contributions across code, docs, security, or community; reviewed by a maintainer; passes OSC contribution-ladder expectations |
| Maintainer | Owner/lead of the repository | Sustained Trusted Committer work; demonstrated governance adherence; OSC approval |

A second maintainer spot is open for a community Trusted Committer per the
[MRP two-spot structure](https://committees.docs.intersectmbo.org/intersect-open-source-committee/about/paid-open-source-model-posm/maintainer-retainer).
Interested candidates: open an issue or contact security@refracticlabs.io.
