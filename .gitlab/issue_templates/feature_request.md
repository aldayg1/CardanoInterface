## Problem

## Proposed solution

## Alignment
Does this align with CardanoInterface's goal of being a local, sovereign wallet
tool with no hosted services required? Does it help users of sunset wallet
providers recover or manage their funds?

## Alternatives considered
What other approaches exist? Why is this one better?

## Scope notes
Feature development is a secondary (20-30%) MRP deliverable. Small, clearly
scoped features are more likely to be picked up.
