# Governance

CardanoInterface is led by its maintainer (see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md),
[CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS)) — Refractic Labs, Founding CTO.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day: maintainer decides, in the open via issues/MRs.
- Direction changes: proposed as an issue, decided within 7 days.
- Single-maintainer project today: no votes to count. When a second
  Trusted Committer joins, decisions move to lazy consensus
  (silence = consent after 72h).

## Roles (Contribution Ladder, short version)

- **Contributor**: merged MR or sustained issue triage.
- **Trusted Committer**: regular quality contributions over 3+ months;
  gains merge rights. Promoted by maintainer, announced in CHANGELOG.
- **Maintainer**: owns releases and security response.

## Money

No sub-treasuries, no re-granting, no token. Funding (grants,
retainers) is disclosed in-repo when received. Financial decisions
follow the same open-issue rule above.
